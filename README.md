## Avies Waypoints

Set waypoints in your world and find your way back to them. This client-side Fabric mod shows your waypoints in the world and lets you share them with other players in chat. Optionally, you can also share them with players who use Xaero's Minimap, they can add your waypoints directly.

### Features

- Create waypoints with a name, initials, coordinates, and one of 21 colors (B for a new waypoint, U for the waypoint list).
- Waypoints are shown in the world with their distance, saved per world, server, and dimension.
- Share waypoints in chat and add shared ones with one click, compatible with Xaero's Minimap.
- Select several waypoints in the list to hide, show, or delete them at once.
- Death waypoints: your last death is marked as "Latest Death", earlier ones are kept as "Old Death #1", "#2", ...
- Optional: player heads instead of colored dots on the locator bar above the hotbar, with your waypoints shown on it too.
- Adjust the display: when the distance and name are shown, size, opacity, maximum distance, and all keybinds.

Requires [TabbyLib](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tabbylib/)
